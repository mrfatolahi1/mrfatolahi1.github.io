§03 What that meant

Responsibilities at Tabdeal

  1. 01

    Platform ownership

    Own all four services behind the exchange's futures market-making platform, taking coverage from zero to ~50 markets at effectively 100% uptime — millions of orders a day against multi-million-dollar balances, where a single error is a direct financial loss.

  2. 02

    Hedge engine

    Built and own the engine that mirrors positions across external exchanges for ~25 markets with exact accounting reconciliation, letting the desk market-make those books with no directional risk.

  3. 03

    Risk manager

    Proposed running the remaining ~25 markets unhedged under algorithmic risk control instead of hedging every position, then designed and built the service that executes it — several thousand dollars of profit within five days from one of the thinnest markets alone.

  4. 04

    Price service

    A sharded streaming service consuming ~30 concurrent WebSocket feeds across three exchanges with REST fallback and per-shard health checks, keeping order-book and mark-price data fresh for every downstream consumer.

  5. 05

    Order placement

    Designed the depth and spread algorithms that quote near-zero spreads across all covered markets.

  6. 06

    Reconciliation

    Traced recurring account discrepancies through every price-mutation source, then automated the fix with reconciliation jobs, balance-mismatch alerts, and daily reports.

  7. 07

    Team leadership

    Deputy to the tech lead — architecture decisions, code review, on-call. Onboarded, mentored, and managed the task flow of two junior engineers.

  8. 08

    AI-first delivery

    Drove the team's AI-first workflow: brainstorm architecture with Claude, generate implementations against detailed plans, review every line given the financial stakes — roughly 60% off delivery time. Authored the shared AI guideline files now shipped in every team repository.

  9. 09

    Product ownership

    Operates as a technical product manager alongside engineering: sets market-maker strategy and represents the team in board and CX meetings.

  10. 10

    Incident leadership

    Led response to the mispricing event overleaf — and shipped the systems that stopped it recurring.

§04 The one that mattered

A mispricing event caused erroneous liquidations.

Led the response: stabilized the affected markets, then made every affected user whole. Then shipped the things that meant it could not happen twice — a pre-deploy sanity-check service, hardened price validation, and a single source of truth for symbol metadata.

Since thenNo recurrence.
